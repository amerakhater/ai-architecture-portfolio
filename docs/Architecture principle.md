## Observability

**Principle:** The system must monitor AI agent activities, workflow execution, errors, and campaign outcomes, with appropriate logging, alerts, and recovery mechanisms.

**Business Goal:** Minimize campaign disruptions, identify issues quickly, and maintain reliable marketing operations.

## Scalability

**Principle:** The system must be scalable to handle increasing work load

**Business Goal:** Support business growth, enable faster introduction of new marketing capabilities, and reduce the cost of future changes.

## Data privacy and security

**Principle:** The system must be secure to protect users’ data

**Business Goal:** Build customer trust, protect sensitive information, and reduce security and regulatory risks.

## Human over-sight for AI decision

**Principle:** The system must not take big decisions alone

**Business Goal:** Ensure that everything is according to the companies liking, so we don’t have false influence that the company doesn’t wish for
